What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in your browser. They are commonly used to remember settings, keep a site working properly, or measure how visitors use it. Similar technologies, such as your browser's local storage, work in much the same way.

How this site uses them

This is a personal portfolio website. It does not use advertising cookies, and it does not sell or share your information with advertisers.

The site does not currently use analytics or tracking cookies. If that changes, this page will be updated to list each cookie, what it is for and how long it lasts.

When you use the contact form, the details you enter (your name, email address and message) are used only to reply to you.

Third-party content

Some pages may load content from other services, such as web fonts, or link to other websites like GitHub. Those services may set their own cookies under their own policies, which I don't control. Please read their policies if you are concerned.

Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies at any time in your browser settings. Most browsers also let you browse in private mode, which clears cookies when you close the window. Blocking cookies should not stop this site from working.

Changes to this policy

I may update this policy if the site changes, for example by adding analytics. The date at the top of the page shows when it was last revised.
